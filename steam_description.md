[h2]⚙️ Sandbox Presets[/h2]

To start a scenario with custom world settings, simply create a Sandbox preset named [b]"pillow"[/b], just like in the original mod.

When starting a new game using one of the scenarios, the mod will automatically load this preset.

[h2]🎲 Scenarios[/h2]

[h3]🚗 Entering Knox County Challenge[/h3]
You are arriving on the outskirts of Knox County with a random amount of gas in your vehicle.

[h3]🏥 Hospital Wake Up Challenge[/h3]
You wake up drugged, hungry, thirsty, groggy, and with a broken bone! You are only wearing a hospital gown, perfect for the modern classic "Wake up after the apocalypse" roleplay.

[h3]🩲 (Almost) Naked and Afraid[/h3]
Who knows how you ended up this way, but you wake up with no clothes and you're terrified out of your mind in a vacant property or cabin.

[h3]🪖 Abandoned Soldier[/h3]
Your squad never showed up, and all of that gunfire you heard must have attracted a nearby horde. You have decent gear, supplies, and some empty/full magazines in your backpack.

[h3]🔒 Prison Challenge[/h3]
Start in your cell with a potentially zombified cellmate and a random tool they smuggled in for you. Escape with your life!

[h3]🏢 Last Ditch Security[/h3]
Start in the March Ridge Dormitory on the second floor, with the first-floor stairs removed.

[h3]✈️ The Final Flight[/h3]
Start as a crash-landed aircraft pilot with some injuries, but also your emergency supplies.

[h3]🔥 Fire Sale[/h3]
Start in the middle of the mall, but several electrical fires break out. The mall alarm may also go off.

[h3]🧪 The Test Subject[/h3]
Something doesn't feel right. You wake in a cave, at the bottom of a massive secret military base.
